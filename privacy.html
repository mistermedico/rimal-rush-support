Privacy Policy

Effective 24 October 2026 · Publisher: Nitai Shore

English

Rimal Rush does not require an account, sign-in, purchase, or subscription. The current release does not include advertising, analytics, or a developer-operated backend, and the developer does not collect personal data through the app.

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. You can erase them using the in-game Delete Local Data control or by uninstalling the app. Locally deleted data cannot be restored by the developer.

The App Store and operating system are provided by Apple and may process information under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. If you contact support, the information you choose to send is used only to answer your request.
